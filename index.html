Wave Crackers Scaling Formula
Wave cracker earnings scale dynamically per wave according to the game's exact Luau formula:
Crackers = ((1.10^Wave − 1) / 10) × Multiplier
• Base Multiplier: 1.0×
• Extra Crackers Pass: +0.50× (+50%)  |  Roblox Premium: +0.15× (+15%)  |  Group Member: +0.10× (+10%)
💡 Tip: Toggle your active Gamepasses & Bonuses in Settings (gear icon in the top-right navbar) to apply multipliers automatically!
